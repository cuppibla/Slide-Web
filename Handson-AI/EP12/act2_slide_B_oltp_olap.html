ACT 2 · THE CROSS-SERVICE JUMP

Why is the data even split?

OLTP — Cloud SQL Postgres
runs the business, one row at a time
  • Row store
  • Live operations
  • High concurrency
  • Row-by-row updates
OLAP — BigQuery
answers questions about the business
  • Columnar store
  • Scans billions of rows
  • Heavy aggregation
  • Built for analysis, not updates
The split is why the investigation is cross-service.
